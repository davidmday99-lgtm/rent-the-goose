GUIDE 01 · BABY SHOWERS

How to choose vintage props for a St. Louis baby shower

Choose pieces that do more than fill a corner. A vintage pram can anchor a welcome display, hold cards or gifts, and create a charming portrait setting.

  • Match the prop colors to two or three tones from your invitation.
  • Place the statement piece where natural light reaches it.
  • Leave enough open space for guests to gather and take photos.

GUIDE 02 · FIRST BIRTHDAYS

Make the high chair part of the birthday story

A vintage high chair gives cake-smash photos and first-birthday portraits a warm, nostalgic focal point. Pick a color that complements the cake and backdrop without disappearing into them.

  • Keep decorations clear of trays, straps, and moving parts.
  • Use a simple fabric banner rather than bulky décor.
  • Plan the chair placement before guests arrive.

GUIDE 03 · PHOTOGRAPHY

Three ways to style vintage props for photos

Good photo props add scale, texture, and a sense of story. They should support the subject rather than compete for attention.

  • Use the wagon for flowers, blankets, or a seasonal display.
  • Pair matching rockers for siblings or generational portraits.
  • Let the pram or goose create a playful editorial moment.
